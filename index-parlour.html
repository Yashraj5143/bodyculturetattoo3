One artist, every line

BodyCulture is a one-artist studio. There's no hand-off between a front desk, a designer and whoever happens to be free: the person you talk to at your consultation is the person who draws your design and tattoos it.

That keeps the design close to your idea, and it means one person stays responsible for how your tattoo heals and how it looks years from now.

  • Single-use needles and fresh ink caps for every client
  • Equipment and surfaces sterilised between every session
  • Every design drawn for you, never copied from someone else's skin

Questions

Does getting a tattoo hurt?

A bit. Most people describe it as a hot scratching feeling. Bony or thin-skinned areas like the ribs, ankles and inner arm are more sensitive than the forearm, thigh or shoulder. You can take a short break whenever you need one.

How should I prepare for my appointment?

Sleep well, eat a proper meal before you come in and drink plenty of water. Skip alcohol for 24 hours beforehand, and wear clothes that make the area easy to reach.

How much will my tattoo cost?

It depends on size, detail and placement. Use the price estimator for a rough range, then get an exact price at your free consultation.

Can you cover up an old tattoo?

Often, yes. Send a clear, well-lit photo of the tattoo on WhatsApp. Darker or larger tattoos may need a bigger design to cover them fully, and you'll get an honest answer about what will work.

Do you take walk-ins?

Because one artist does every tattoo, booked appointments come first. Message on WhatsApp to check whether there's a same-day slot.

How old do I need to be?

You need to be 18 or older, and you'll be asked to show a valid photo ID.

How do I look after a new tattoo?

Keep it clean, wash it gently with mild soap and apply a thin layer of the recommended balm. For the first two weeks, avoid swimming, direct sun and picking at any flaking skin. You'll get written instructions on the day.
