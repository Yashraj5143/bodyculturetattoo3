Questions

Does getting a tattoo hurt?

A bit. Most people describe it as a hot scratching feeling. Use the planner above to see how different spots compare. You can take a short break whenever you need one.

How should I prepare for my appointment?

Sleep well, eat a proper meal before you come in and drink plenty of water. Skip alcohol for 24 hours beforehand, and wear clothes that make the area easy to reach.

Can you cover up an old tattoo?

Often, yes. Send a clear, well-lit photo of the tattoo on WhatsApp. Darker or larger tattoos may need a bigger design to cover them fully, and you'll get an honest answer about what will work.

What styles do you do?

Fine line, blackwork and mandala, realism and portraits, spiritual designs, lettering, colour and cover-ups. Filter the work above by style to see examples.

Do you take walk-ins?

Because one artist does every tattoo, booked appointments come first. Message on WhatsApp to check whether there's a same-day slot.

How old do I need to be?

You need to be 18 or older, and you'll be asked to show a valid photo ID.

How do I look after a new tattoo?

Keep it clean, wash it gently with mild soap and apply a thin layer of the recommended balm. For the first two weeks, avoid swimming, direct sun and picking at any flaking skin. You'll get written instructions on the day.

How is the final price decided?

By size, detail and placement. The planner gives you a rough range, and the exact price is agreed at your free consultation before anything starts.
